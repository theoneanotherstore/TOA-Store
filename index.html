Good to know

Questions

How does ordering work?

Pick a product and size, upload your photos if it is a Customization product, add a short note if you like, and pay securely with Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking). We check your photos with you on WhatsApp before printing.

Which photos can I upload?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF files, up to 10 per product and 50 MB each. Customised polaroids need at least 5 designs and customised stickers at least 10. Customised UNO cards need two PDFs: one with all card designs (front and back) and one for the packaging. We will message you if a photo needs changing.

How long does delivery take?

Custom orders are printed and delivered in 5–7 working days after we confirm your photos and payment.

Can I return or cancel a custom order?

Custom products are non-refundable and non-returnable. If something arrives damaged or wrong, message us on WhatsApp straight away.

How do I track my order?

Go to Orders in the navigation bar, then enter your order ID and the UTR, payment ID or tracking ID we sent you on WhatsApp or email. You can also choose Your orders and enter your email and phone number to see all your orders. We send every update on WhatsApp or email.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, the minimum order is ₹249.
